How Personalized Recommendations Improved the Player Experience on Boost Casino

Boost Casino moved from a manually curated, static lobby to a personalized and adaptive game layout — delivering a sustained uplift in turnover, GGR, and player engagement without disrupting their existing UX.

01 · Overview

Overview

Boost Casino is a Baltic-Nordic gaming brand providing a 'pay & play' experience within the Entain family. ZingBrain AI is the solution that delivers personalized game recommendations inside the casino lobby.

Boost Casino aimed to move from a manually curated, static lobby to a personalized and adaptive game layout. The goal was to improve key performance metrics while reducing the amount of ongoing manual work.

Working closely together, the two teams introduced two core personalization features: a recommendations section on the main casino page and a "similar games" pop-up shown when a player closes a game. To accurately measure the impact, the teams ran an A/B test — one group continued to see the original lobby, while the other received the personalized experience.

02 · Collaboration

Collaboration

Boost Casino's key objective was to move away from a single, uniform way of sorting games in the lobby and instead deliver more relevant content to each individual player. This approach aimed to broaden players' game portfolios, strengthen loyalty to the brand, and improve core monetary metrics.

Through close collaboration between both product teams, the companies found an optimal way to introduce personalization into the existing lobby without disrupting the brand's already strong UX. At the same time, the personalized sections were placed in highly visible positions to maximize impact.

Using the Zing back-office tools, the partner quickly understood the API structure, the personalized outputs, and the required data. Despite a complex internal architecture and multiple platform dependencies, the integration was completed quickly and rolled out without critical issues.

The final stage focused on evaluating results. In-depth analytics — including percentile-based analysis and the CUPED method — clearly demonstrated the uplift and helped the partner make an objective decision to continue the collaboration and further expand personalization across the product.

03 · The Solution

Two personalization modules

Two personalization modules were introduced on the brand to cover both lobby discovery and in-session game continuation.

01

Main Recommended Section

The core personalized module that reflects each player's key preferences. It combines familiar titles the player already enjoys with new games they haven't discovered yet.

  • For every player, the system calculates a Discovery Score — a measure of how willing they are to try new games — and adapts the ratio of new vs. known titles accordingly.
  • Post-processing is applied to ensure recommendations stay fresh and do not repeat too often.
02

Similar Games Pop-Up on Game Exit

When a player closes a game, before returning them to the lobby, they see a pop-up with titles similar to the one they just played.

  • Similarity is calculated using a wide range of parameters: game type, features, theme, volatility, and collaborative filtering signals.
  • Surfaces the most relevant alternatives for every game available on the brand.

04 · Results

Results analysis

Because turnover and GGR are highly volatile metrics, it was important to approach the analysis in a comprehensive way and isolate the real impact of personalization — independent of seasonality, traffic fluctuations, or other external factors.

Several analytical methods were used: trimming groups by percentiles, applying the CUPED method, reviewing median per-player values for long-term players, and checking statistical significance through p-values. Based on the joint analysis, the conclusion was clear.

↑

Uplift in turnover per player confirmed via A/B test across both player groups

↑

Uplift in GGR per player with consistency maintained over following months

↑

Slight uplift in unique games played per player — the key proxy metric for engagement depth

#1

Recommendation modules became the leading source of game discovery on the brand over time

06 · Next Steps

Conclusion & next steps

The introduction of personalized recommendations proved to be a meaningful step forward for Boost Casino, delivering a clear and sustained uplift in key performance metrics while strengthening the overall player experience.

The collaboration showed that personalization can be integrated quickly, safely, and with measurable impact — even within a complex platform structure.

Looking ahead, both sides plan to expand personalization across additional touchpoints, explore new real-time signals, and test more advanced discovery scenarios — unlocking even deeper engagement and a continuously evolving, player-centric casino experience.
