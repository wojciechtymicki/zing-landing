Case Study · Hondubet

No More Manual Management — The Lobby Runs Itself

01 · Overview

Overview

Hondubet is a fast-growing brand with a strong focus on product quality and player experience. The team constantly looks for ways to improve the platform and make the lobby more relevant for their audience.

They had two clear objectives: turn the lobby from a static, one-size-fits-all layout into a dynamic experience that adapts to each player in real time, and minimize manual lobby management while keeping human control only at the strategic level.

To make this happen, Hondubet partnered with ZingBrain AI and rebuilt the way their casino lobby works from the ground up.

02 · The Challenge

The challenge

Before the transformation, the lobby relied heavily on manual collections and static sections. This created familiar challenges that the team wanted to solve permanently.

  • — The same structure for every player, regardless of behavior or preferences
  • — Constant manual updates across multiple lobby sections
  • — Irrelevant content and duplicate games appearing across different sections
  • — Ongoing time investment from the casino operations team
  • — Limited flexibility when promoting specific providers or commercial deals

03 · The Solution

ZingBrain AI as the lobby engine

ZingBrain AI became the engine behind the Hondubet lobby. Today, almost all lobby sections are powered directly through the Zing API, adapting automatically as player behavior changes.

01

Fully API-Driven Lobby Sections

Instead of manually building and updating collections, Hondubet dynamically pulls personalized sections that reflect each player's behavior and preferences. No duplicates. No irrelevant games.

  • Favorite Mechanic & Favorite Provider sections
  • Games the player hasn't tried yet
  • Similar games based on the player's top titles
  • Trending games and top games among other players
  • A pop-up with similar games when a player closes a game
02

Smart Promotion Without Breaking Personalization

Like any operator, Hondubet needs to promote certain games or providers due to commercial agreements, campaigns, or strategic priorities. Using Zing's Game Promotion tool, this is achieved without manual intervention or sacrificing relevance.

  • Promoted titles are ranked per player and integrated into personalized sections
  • Shown consistently only if they match the player's interests
  • If a player doesn't engage, titles naturally fade out and are replaced by relevant options

In practice, there are almost no sections left that require daily manual updates. The operational load on the casino team dropped significantly, and the constant "lobby maintenance" bottleneck disappeared.

06 · Conclusion

A self-optimizing, data-driven lobby

For Hondubet, this project was not only about improving KPIs. It was about building a modern casino experience where the lobby adapts to the player — not the other way around.

By combining automation, intelligent promotion logic, and real-time personalization, the brand moved from manual curation to a self-optimizing, data-driven lobby. Personalization is no longer an add-on — it is the core logic of the Hondubet lobby.

  • +Radical reduction in manual lobby management overhead
  • +No duplicate or irrelevant games across sections
  • +Seamless promotion of priority providers and titles
  • +Nearly 100% of active players interacting with recommendation sections
  • +Scalable system supporting both player satisfaction and commercial objectives
