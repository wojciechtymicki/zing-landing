Case Study · CatCasino

Turning Game Discovery into a Promotion Engine

CatCasino introduced personalized recommendations to help experienced players discover new titles naturally — while simultaneously turning the same recommendation engine into a powerful, player-first promotion tool that delivered +40% GGR growth for promoted games.

01 · Overview

Overview

CatCasino is a well-known brand with an active and experienced player base. Many of their players are already familiar with a large portion of the game catalog and are constantly looking for new titles to try.

The team wanted to introduce personalization into the lobby to better adapt to these player behaviors and make game discovery easier. At the same time, CatCasino works closely with game providers and regularly promotes specific titles as part of commercial partnerships.

The goal was to find a balance where promotional placements would not disrupt personalization or reduce player engagement — while still delivering measurable growth in the performance of promoted games. To achieve this, CatCasino partnered with ZingBrain AI to introduce personalized game recommendations directly into the player journey.

02 · The Challenge

The challenge

Before introducing personalization, the lobby relied mostly on traditional sections and manually managed placements — creating limitations that affected both players and the business.

  • — Experienced players had limited support when searching for new games
  • — Finding new games depended heavily on static sections and manual sorting
  • — Promoted titles often required top placements that could affect lobby relevance for some players
  • — The team needed a way to promote games without negatively impacting engagement or key metrics

03 · The Solution

Three recommendation touchpoints

To introduce personalization in a way that felt natural to players, the teams implemented three key recommendation touchpoints — covering lobby discovery, in-session exploration, and commercial promotion.

01

"Suggested for You" Section on the Main Page

A personalized "Suggested for You" section was placed at the very top of the main casino lobby, becoming the primary place where players start choosing what to play next.

  • Instead of showing the same games to every user, the system ranks games individually for each player based on behavior and preferences
  • For experienced players constantly exploring new titles, this section makes it significantly easier to discover relevant games they have not yet tried
  • Naturally adapts to each player's interests as their behavior evolves over time
02

Similar Games Pop-Up on Game Exit

To support continuous game exploration during a session, CatCasino also introduced a Similar Games pop-up that appears when a player closes a game.

  • Instead of returning directly to the lobby, players are shown several games with similar mechanics, themes, or gameplay features
  • Creates a smooth discovery flow and helps players move naturally from one game to another without breaking the session
03

Smart Game Promotion Through the Zing Backoffice

Using the Zing Game Promotion tool in the backoffice, casino managers add specific games to the promotion list each month. Once added, these games are automatically integrated into both recommendation modules.

  • In the Main Recommended section, promoted games appear in order of their relevance to each individual player
  • In the Similar Games pop-up, promoted titles can appear at top positions if they are genuinely similar to the game the player just played
  • Promotional visibility is achieved naturally, without forcing irrelevant titles into the player's experience

05 · Promoted Games

Impact on promoted games

The introduction of the Zing Game Promotion Tool significantly changed how promotional titles perform on CatCasino.

Previously, most GGR uplift came only from games placed in the top 5–10 positions in the lobby. With personalized and rotation-based promotion, CatCasino was able to promote 100+ games simultaneously — exposing them to players for whom those titles were actually relevant.

As a result, promoted games began receiving traffic from new and more suitable player segments, leading to consistent growth in both turnover and GGR across the entire promotion list.

07 · Conclusion

A balanced ecosystem for players and the business

For CatCasino, personalization was not just a case of adding another feature to the lobby. It was about building a smarter way to explore games that works for both players and the business.

By combining personalized recommendations with intelligent promotion logic, the brand created a lobby where players can easily discover new games while promotional titles still receive strong visibility.

The result is a balanced ecosystem where player engagement and commercial objectives grow together, without compromising the gaming experience.

  • +Main Recommended section became the most popular place for players to launch games
  • +Increased turnover and GGR across all games in the promotion tool
  • +Strong and growing engagement with both recommendation modules
  • +Promotion and personalization working together instead of competing
