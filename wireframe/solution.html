Provider Cost Optimization

Optimize provider costs inside personalized recommendations. ZingBrain AI lets operators set visibility priorities for providers and games, so personalized recommendations can support both player relevance and commercial efficiency.

01 · Overview

What it does

Provider Cost Optimization adds a commercial control layer to ZingBrain AI recommendations.

Operators can increase or decrease the visibility of selected providers and games based on commercial terms, active campaigns, new game launches, or regional business goals.

The player still receives personalized recommendations. The difference is that the model also understands which content should receive more or less exposure from the operator's business perspective.

02 · Context

Why it matters

Casino games can look similar to players, but they can have very different commercial value for operators.

Some providers have better terms, some games are part of active campaigns, and some content is more important in specific countries.

Without this layer, the lobby often follows pure engagement and popularity signals. That can make popular games even more dominant, while commercially important content gets less visibility.

Provider Cost Optimization helps operators guide traffic toward more efficient games and providers without turning the lobby into a static promo board.

03 · Mechanics

How it works

Operators set priority levels for providers and individual games in the Backoffice. Priorities can be applied globally or configured separately for specific countries.

ZingBrain AI still ranks games by player relevance, but uses priority settings as an additional business signal. Teams can validate the impact through A/B testing and analytics.

  • + Set provider and game-level priorities in the Backoffice
  • + Apply globally or override per country
  • + Personalized ranking is preserved — priorities act as an additional signal
  • + Validate impact through built-in A/B testing and analytics

04 · Configuration

Priority levels

Five priority levels control how much exposure a provider or game gets inside personalized recommendations.

Very High

Strong visibility boost for the most commercially important games or providers.

High

Moderate visibility boost for strategic content or active campaigns.

Neutral

Default personalized ranking without commercial adjustment.

Low

Reduced visibility for less efficient providers or games.

Very Low

Strongest visibility reduction for the lowest-priority content.
